# Research Proposal

**Title:** Does Screen Time Before Bed Affect Sleep Quality in College Students?

## 1. Problem
Many students use phones and laptops right before sleeping. Poor sleep is linked to lower focus and worse wellbeing, so it is useful to know whether bedtime screen use is related to sleep quality.

## 2. Literature Review (brief)
Prior studies suggest that screen light and stimulating content can delay sleep onset and reduce sleep quality. Findings on the *size* of the effect are mixed, and many studies use small or single-campus samples. *(Replace with 3–5 real sources, e.g. from Google Scholar.)*

## 3. Research Question
Is more screen time in the hour before bed associated with lower self-reported sleep quality among college students?

## 4. Hypotheses
- **H0:** There is no relationship between bedtime screen time and sleep quality.
- **H1:** More bedtime screen time is associated with lower sleep quality.

## 5. Methodology (summary)
Cross-sectional online survey of college students. Screen time (hours) and sleep quality (1–10 scale) are self-reported. Analysis: descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation, and a t-test with effect size. See `02_data_collection_plan.md` and `03_analysis_methodology.md`.

## 6. Limitations
Self-reported data, correlation is not causation, convenience sampling may limit generalisation.
